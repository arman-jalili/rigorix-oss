```

cargo audit --deny warnings          after the bump       → exit 0

It now runs the pipeline's command, prints the crate and the advisory id, and fails with the remedy (bump, or a suppression with an expiry in `.cargo/audit.toml` — a decision, not a silence). Mirrored to all five crates; drift check clean.
